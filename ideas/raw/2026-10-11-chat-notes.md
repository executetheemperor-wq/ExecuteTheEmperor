## Note 1 — the three phases

> Different phases.
>
> Phase: finding co-conspirators, trying to find people to join the cause. Hiring them or charming them or blackmailing them. Different values of like hate, the more you have them in your grasp the more they either need to be part of it or not. Can execute them or leave them alive, but if they rat you out you have a chance of being ended
>
> Phase: planning. Choosing to kill the emporer, certain ways beget certain bonuses, you can acquire funding to do it in a certain way. Map of imperial palace, parts can be obscured due to building works, not having the plans etc. Get closer to the emporer to be able to do more in planning phase, locking certain doors, replacing certain guards with ones in your post, etc.
>
> Phase: the murder. You can get to see it if you're there, but you may not be able to. If you're there, which increases suspicion, you get to see exactly how people act, if you're not there you have to rely on word of mouth, and plots can be growing or not depending on how you interpret some people's reports.
>
> Then restart. Need to kill certain number of emporers to be crowned yourself.
>
>
> Forum shadowy circle, where discuss the plot in first phase.
>
> Map of imperial palace in second phase
>
> Reports in third phase
