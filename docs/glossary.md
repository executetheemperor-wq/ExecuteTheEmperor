# Glossary

Working vocabulary from the notes. *(proposed)* marks names picked for this
repo rather than words from the notes — rename freely.

## The game

| Term | Meaning | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| **Run** | One plot against one Emperor, from first recruit to the night of the murder (or failure). | [Run structure](design/01-run-structure.md) |
| **Phase** | A stage of a run: Political, Conspiracy, Plotting/Planning, Night of the murder. | [Run structure](design/01-run-structure.md) |
| **Turn** | The unit of time; each one adds 1 Pressure. *(Not yet defined.)* | [Pressure](design/02-pressure.md) |
| **Path / pathing** | Slay the Spire-style choice of upcoming events, with limited foresight. | [Cards, events and pathing](design/05-cards-events-and-pathing.md) |

## Numbers

| Term | Meaning | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| **Pressure** | The 0–100 master clock. Easy to gain, very hard to lose; ~80 = point of no return; 100 = autopilot. | [Pressure](design/02-pressure.md) |
| **Variable / stat** | Any 0–100 number on you, an NPC or the Emperor; average ~50. | [Stats](design/03-stats-and-checks.md) |
| **Global / local variable** | Global: applies to the player, changed by anything. Local: applies to the player or an NPC, changed only by them. | [Stats](design/03-stats-and-checks.md) |
| **Check** | d100 roll-under: roll ≤ stat to pass (Call of Cthulhu). | [Stats](design/03-stats-and-checks.md) |
| **Happiness** | How content a co-conspirator is with you. Up when you play their cards. | [Co-conspirators](design/04-co-conspirators.md) |
| **Suspicion** | How much someone (or Rome, or the Senate) suspects you. | [Stats](design/03-stats-and-checks.md) |
| **Gullibility** | How much someone thinks they can get away with lying to you. | [Stats](design/03-stats-and-checks.md) |
| **Notice** | How much the Emperor is paying attention to you. | [The Emperor](design/06-the-emperor.md) |
| **Influence** | A core player stat; gates area-plan options and influence rolls. | [Stats](design/03-stats-and-checks.md) |
| **Public / Senate relations** | Your Likeability, Trust, Suspicion and Virtue with Rome and with the Senate. | [Politics](design/08-politics-and-reputation.md) |

## People

| Term | Meaning | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| **Co-conspirator** | A recruit to the plot, represented as a card. | [Co-conspirators](design/04-co-conspirators.md) |
| **Draft pool** | The changing selection of co-conspirators you can recruit; persists between rounds. | [Co-conspirators](design/04-co-conspirators.md) |
| **Trait** | A co-conspirator's (or Emperor's) quirk that bends how stats affect them — e.g. socialite, military man. | [Stats](design/03-stats-and-checks.md) |
| **Sticky** | Co-conspirators are very hard to remove once they know the plan. | [Co-conspirators](design/04-co-conspirators.md) |
| **Grasp** | From the first note: how tightly you hold someone; the tighter, the more they "need to be part of it". | [Co-conspirators](design/04-co-conspirators.md) |
| **Rat you out** | A co-conspirator betrays you — a chance you're ended. | [Co-conspirators](design/04-co-conspirators.md) |
| **The Shadowy Circle** | The forum where the conspiracy meets and discusses the plot. | [Co-conspirators](design/04-co-conspirators.md) |
| **Key figures** | Rivals vying for power alongside you. | [Politics](design/08-politics-and-reputation.md) |

## Cards

| Term | Meaning | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| **Card pool** | Your cards plus your co-conspirators', grouped by type (e.g. military); events draw from the matching pool. | [Cards](design/05-cards-events-and-pathing.md) |
| **Curse** | A negative card a co-conspirator brings with them. | [Cards](design/05-cards-events-and-pathing.md) |
| **One-time card** | A single-use card playable outside events, e.g. a propaganda campaign. | [Cards](design/05-cards-events-and-pathing.md) |
| **Intel card** | A known weakness of the Emperor, e.g. *Creature of Habit*. | [The Emperor](design/06-the-emperor.md) |
| **Emperor Sheet** | Where everything you've learned about the Emperor is recorded. | [The Emperor](design/06-the-emperor.md) |

## The murder

| Term | Meaning | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| **Area** | Where the Emperor is killed — banquet, Colosseum, gardens… | [Assassination](design/07-the-assassination.md) |
| **Method** | How he's killed. Certain ways beget certain bonuses. | [Assassination](design/07-the-assassination.md) |
| **Area plan** | The chessboard set-up: doors, spies, bribes, guards, conspirators. | [Assassination](design/07-the-assassination.md) |
| **Kill zone** | Where you want the Emperor's route to take him. | [Assassination](design/07-the-assassination.md) |
| **Reports** | Second-hand accounts of the night, which are all you have if you weren't there. | [Assassination](design/07-the-assassination.md) |
| **Foiled** | The plot fails; a chance you're executed. | [Assassination](design/07-the-assassination.md) |
| **Ides** | Bonus event: harder, but less suspicion on co-conspirators. | [Cards](design/05-cards-events-and-pathing.md) |

## Reputation and meta

| Term | Meaning | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| **Honorific** | A reputation earned from events or public rating, e.g. "cheater". Also a stat. | [Politics](design/08-politics-and-reputation.md) |
| **Propaganda campaign** | Costs gold; shifts public or Senate relations. | [Politics](design/08-politics-and-reputation.md) |
| **Lesson** | One of three choices after a failed run, tied to why it failed. | [Between runs](design/09-between-runs.md) |
| **Permanent upgrade** | A lasting boost from a lesson, capped per area (DD2 candles of hope). | [Between runs](design/09-between-runs.md) |
| **Crowned** | The long-term win: kill enough Emperors and take the throne. | [Between runs](design/09-between-runs.md) |
| **Pressure tokens** | Board game: they build up; at a value you must plot. | [Board game](design/10-board-game.md) |
