# Paper prototype — playtest log

One entry per game, newest first. If you change a rule, note it here *and*
in [rules.md](rules.md) or [cards.md](cards.md).

## What we're trying to learn

1. Is drafting co-conspirators interesting — are there real choices?
2. Does Pressure feel like your crew pushing you — a reason to strike, and
   scary to wait?
3. Does a sticky crew feel like a fun problem, or just a punishment?
4. Is there enough to do each turn?
5. How long does a turn take? The target is about 30 seconds
   ([D3](../decisions.md)).
