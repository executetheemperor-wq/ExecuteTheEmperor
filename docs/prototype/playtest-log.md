# Paper prototype — playtest log

One entry per game, newest first. If you change a rule, note it here *and*
in [rules.md](rules.md) or [cards.md](cards.md).

## What we're trying to learn

Questions 1–5 follow the [research prompt](../research/deep-research-prompt.md#game-brief)'s
first five. The follow-ups are the measures the
[research](../research/2026-10-12-comparable-games.md#6-implications-for-execute-the-emperor)
suggests.

1. **The draft.** Is drafting co-conspirators interesting — are there real
   choices? Did you ever recruit someone *for* their bad card?
2. **Pressure.** Does it feel like your crew pushing you (a reason to
   strike, and scary to wait), or just a timer? Would you describe it as
   "the crew" or "the clock"? Did you Push?
3. **The sticky crew.** Does it feel like a fun problem, or just a
   punishment? Did you act with a bad card? Did you lie low?
4. **The turn.** Is there enough to do? Did you stop to think about which
   card to act with? How long did a turn take? The target is about 30
   seconds ([D3](../decisions.md)).
5. **The strike.** Could you say your odds before you rolled? Did the win
   (or the loss) feel earned?
