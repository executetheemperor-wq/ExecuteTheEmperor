# Paper prototype v0.1 — cards

> **Claude's first draft** of every card for the [paper prototype](rules.md).
> Archetypes, the three named intel cards and a few effects come from your
> notes (cited); names, numbers and the rest are mine — rewrite freely.
> 69 cards: 8 starter, 10 co-conspirators, 30 co-conspirator cards, 15
> events, 6 secrets.

**On every card:** Money and Influence are spent this turn. Charm, Military
and Political are your lasting stats (0–10). Pressure and Suspicion are the
tracks on your sheet. Happiness sits on each co-conspirator.

## Co-conspirators (10)

Each one is a card for the market, plus the three cards they bring. Write
the owner's name on each of their cards, so you know whose Happiness it
moves.

| Tier | Who | Enters the market |
|---|---|---|
| **Willing** | Aurelia, Gaius, Marcus, Felix, Livia | From the start |
| **Ambitious** | Sextus, Titus, Cassius | When Pressure first reaches 6 |
| **Unstable** | Decimus, Vibia — both **Uninvited** | When Pressure first reaches 12 |

**Access** on co-conspirators: Palace · Blade · Streets · Actors · Poison ·
Gold · Praetorians. *(The plans in OneNote › 3.3 need someone with access to
an area, someone with access to the right people, and the means.)*

## The Emperor's secrets (6)

Three are dealt face down each game. When one is revealed, it opens a
**route** for [the strike](rules.md#the-strike): its two stats, plus 1 for
each co-conspirator in on the night with matching access.

| Secret | What you've learned | Route | Stats | Access that helps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Creature of Habit** | "They follow a strict routine, and can always be found doing certain things at certain times." *(OneNote › The night of the murder)* | Ambush on his walk | Military + Political | Blade, Palace |
| **The Praetorians Never Leave Him** | "He spends his evenings at the palace heavily guarded with the praetorian guard." *(OneNote › 3.3)* | Turn the guard | Military + Political | Praetorians, Gold |
| **Partially Blind** | "They have a blind spot due to blindness in one eye." *(OneNote › The night of the murder)* | From his blind side | Military + Charm | Blade, Streets |
| **Loves the Crowd** | *Mine:* he walks among the people on feast days — a "Public Outing". *(OneNote › Areas)* | In the crowd | Military + Charm | Streets, Gold |
| **Take My Wife, Please** | "They have a proclivity to trust women above all else." *(OneNote › The night of the murder)* | Through the Empress's household | Charm + Political | Palace, Poison |
| **A Taste for the Stage** | "At night he covertly goes to watch plays, indulge in wine." *(OneNote › 3.3)* | Poison at the theatre | Charm + Political | Actors, Poison |

Each pair of stats has two routes, so whatever crew you've built, a
revealed secret might suit it — or might not.
