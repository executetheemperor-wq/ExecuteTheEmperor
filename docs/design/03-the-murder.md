# Phase 3 — The Murder

**Centrepiece:** Reports
**Job:** the plan plays out — and you find out what *really* happened, or
what people say happened.

## Your notes

> Phase: the murder. You can get to see it if you're there, but you may not be able to. If you're there, which increases suspicion, you get to see exactly how people act, if you're not there you have to rely on word of mouth, and plots can be growing or not depending on how you interpret some people's reports.
>
> Reports in third phase

## Consolidated

### Be there, or don't

You **can** see the murder if you're there — "but you may not be able to"
(*be there at all, or see it even when you are? See open question 17.*)

| | You're there | You're not there |
|---|---|---|
| **What you learn** | You see **exactly how people act**. | You **rely on word of mouth**. |
| **Cost** | **Increases suspicion.** | You only know what you're told. |

### Reports

The phase is told through **reports**.

If you weren't there, the reports are all you have — and **plots can be
growing or not depending on how you interpret some people's reports.**
