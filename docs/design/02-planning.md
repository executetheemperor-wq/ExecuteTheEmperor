## Consolidated

### Choosing the method

You choose **how** to kill the Emperor.

- **Certain ways beget certain bonuses** — the method you pick matters.
- **Funding** — you can acquire funding to do it in a certain way.

### The palace map

The phase is played on a **map of the Imperial Palace**.

**Parts can be obscured**, for example by:

- building works
- not having the plans

### Getting closer to the Emperor

The **closer you get to the Emperor, the more you can do** in planning.
Example actions:

- **Lock certain doors**
- **Replace certain guards** with ones in your post *(your own people? — see open question 16)*
- *etc.* — the list is open-ended
