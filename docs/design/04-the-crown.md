# The Crown — restart and win condition

## Your notes

> Then restart. Need to kill certain number of emporers to be crowned yourself.

## Consolidated

After the murder, **the game restarts** with a new Emperor and the three
phases begin again:

```
Conspiracy  →  Planning  →  The Murder  →  (new Emperor)  →  Conspiracy  → …
```

**Win:** kill a **certain number of Emperors** and you are **crowned
yourself**.

**Lose:** a co-conspirator you left alive **rats you out** and the chance
comes up against you — you are **ended** (see
[Phase 1](01-conspiracy.md#execute-or-spare)).
