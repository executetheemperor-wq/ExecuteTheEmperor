# Politics and reputation

Killing the Emperor is only half of it. You're also building a **political
career** — one strong enough that, when he falls, you might be the one who
rises. *(OneNote › Political Phase)*

## The political phase

The Political Phase is where you:

- **learn** about the Emperor, the co-conspirators and the state of politics,
  and use it to build out the plan;
- run into **random events** that add to the Emperor Sheet, give you money
  or other boons, and sway public opinion;
- **bolster your own career**, making it more likely that *you* become
  Emperor.

*(OneNote › Political Phase)*

## Rivals

"Underlying conditions give points to other **key figures**, who also
intersect and are vying for power themselves." You aren't the only one with
ambitions. *(OneNote › Political Phase)*

## The public and the Senate

Your standing is tracked **with Rome as a whole** and **with the Senate as a
whole** — Likeability, Trust, Suspicion and Virtue for each. The Emperor
has his own Senate and Public relationship. *(OneNote › Stats,
1. Variables)*

## Honorifics

Some events, and how the public rates you, can earn you **honorifics** —
reputations that change your public sway and how the world sees you.

> Cheat at a game during an event and you may become known as **a cheater**.
> That changes who you meet and what they'll want before joining the plot —
> you might need to lose a gambling match to them, or hand over a lot of
> money to prove yourself. Or more people with the same streak come looking
> for you.

Both you and the Emperor have an **Honorific** stat. *(OneNote ›
Honorifics, Stats)*

## Campaigns

**Propaganda campaigns** shift your relationship with the **public** or the
**Senate**. They cost **gold**, and how well they work depends on your
honorifics, your other stats and how the public sees you. *(OneNote ›
Propaganda Campaigns)* A co-conspirator can bring a one-time propaganda card
— costs money, raises Pressure, boosts a stat. *(OneNote › 3.2)*

A separate page, *Campaigns*, stops after two words: "Campaigns influence".
*(OneNote › Campaigns)*

## Speeches

A speech **denouncing the Emperor's policy**: +5 Pressure, more Influence,
more suspicion from the Emperor. *(OneNote › 1.1 Pressure)* ("Speech" is
also on the list of places he could be killed. *(OneNote › Areas)*)
