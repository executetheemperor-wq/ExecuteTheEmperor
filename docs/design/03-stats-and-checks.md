# Stats and checks

The game is mostly about **managing numbers** — your own, each
co-conspirator's, the Emperor's — and how they push on each other.
*(OneNote › 1. Variables)*

## The scale

- Every variable runs **0–100**: 0 is abysmal, 100 perfect, and things
  should sit **around 50** on average. *(OneNote › 1. Variables)*
- **Checks are d100 roll-under**, like Call of Cthulhu: with Influence 70,
  roll a d100; 70 or under passes. *(OneNote › 3.1 The Effect of
  Co-Conspirators on Stats)*

## Global and local

The notes split variables into **global** (apply to the player; anything can
change them) and **local** (apply to the player or an NPC; only the player or
NPCs can change them). Pressure is "the most important global variable".
*(OneNote › 1. Variables)*

**Conflict —** both definitions say "apply to the player", so the line
between them isn't clear yet. See [audit](../audit.md).

## Stats push on each other

- **Happiness generally lowers Suspicion** — though someone can be happy with
  you *and* suspicious of you.
- **Money rises with Influence**, in every run.
- **Co-conspirators bring their own rules**, set by their traits. A
  *socialite* likes your Charm and dislikes your militarism — and as they
  warm to you, they think you're gullible and start spinning yarns. A
  *military man* likes you less the more charming you are.
- Their **traits are shown to you**, so you can weigh it up: more Charm to
  please the socialite, at the cost of the soldier?

*(OneNote › 1. Variables)*

## Co-conspirators change your numbers

Every co-conspirator adds their own standing onto your adjusted base stats —
an influential one raises your Influence, which means more and better
Influence rolls. *(OneNote › 3.1 The Effect of Co-Conspirators on Stats)*

## Gaps

- **One stat list.** Merge the two passes; decide what Virtue, Age, Health
  and Honorific actually *do*.
- How **Suspicion** turns into getting caught.
- What raises or lowers **Notice**, and what the Emperor does when it's high.
- Whether "Happiness" (co-conspirators) and "Likeability" (relations) are
  the same thing.
- Gold vs Money — the notes use both.
